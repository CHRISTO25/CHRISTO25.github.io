Christo varghese

I am Christo Varghese, a passionate and results-driven Software Engineer with hands-on experience in full-stack development. I hold a Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) degree from Girideepam Institute of Advanced Learning (GPA: 8.00 / 10.00). I specialize in designing and implementing scalable, efficient, and user-friendly applications with a deep understanding of MVC architecture, RESTful APIs, and database management. Currently, I am working as a Software Engineer Intern at Mentorow in Kakkanad, Kerala, where I lead backend and frontend initiatives using React.js, Redux, Node.js, and MongoDB. I take pride in improving API performance and database optimization, contributing to a 60% increase in API response times. My ability to work across functional teams and implement secure, scalable solutions has resulted in improved product efficiency and client satisfaction. Previously, I interned with Brototype in Bengaluru, where I enhanced my front-end development skills and boosted testing coverage, reducing post-release defects by 25%. I also deployed projects on AWS with Nginx, ensuring improved performance and scalability. Beyond professional roles, I have developed and led impactful projects, such as Zntra.com (an e-commerce platform) and Jobs Land (a job-finding portal), showcasing my expertise in full-stack development, clean architecture, and API integration. These projects reflect my commitment to delivering user-centric solutions with enhanced security and fast response handling. With proficiency in JavaScript, TypeScript, C++, React.js, Express.js, MongoDB, and AWS, I enjoy tackling complex problems with innovative solutions. My goal is to continue evolving as a developer while contributing to meaningful projects that make a difference.
